# RugDealer

Loans secured by finished rugs held at a partner store.

## Language

**Certificate holder**:
The party entitled to collect a free rug from its custody store. A pledged rug remains in custody even though its certificate names a holder.
_Avoid_: Buyer (unless a separate sale actually occurs)

**Loan settlement**:
Closure of a funded loan by repayment, store buy-out or investor claim. Settlement determines the rug's holder and the investor's payout entitlement.
_Avoid_: Payment received

**Held credit**:
A settled payout owed to an investor whose wallet could not receive it. It remains backed by funds until the investor withdraws it.
_Avoid_: Paid, received

**Payout delivery**:
Receipt of the settlement money by the investor, either directly or through a later withdrawal of held credit.
_Avoid_: Loan settlement

**Hand-over**:
Physical release of a rug by its custody store to the confirmed certificate holder after certificate closure.
_Avoid_: Loan repayment, code creation
